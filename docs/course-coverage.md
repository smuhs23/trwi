## Scope limits

Every document group was inspected. This is a thematic transformation into independently worded exercises, not a transcription or interactive equivalent of every original page/question. Tables, diagrams, manufacturer-specific selections, complete sizing cases and practical measuring/inspection skills still require the original material and instruction. All course questions have `draft` status and page references, not independent professional approval. Repetition counts practice, never proves examination readiness. TRWI source documents have not been provided.

Historical legal excerpts and insurance terms are identified as historical documents; quiz results do not establish current legal rules. Contradictions in technical values between main texts and answers are excluded rather than silently resolved. Private audit reports retain the detailed discrepancies without publishing source scans.

## Learning mechanics

Short missions, optional mini explanations, immediate feedback, stable shuffled answer positions per saved session, and scheduled replays. Wrong course answers return today; successful unassisted work is scheduled after 1, 3, then 7 days. A later supported answer in the same round cannot erase an earlier error. Same-day repeat rewards are deduplicated. Existing demo progress and workshop rewards remain valid.

## Delivered size and quarantined conflicts

29 course missions / 172 questions, plus the unchanged 5 introductory missions / 22 questions. Lessons and per-question source references are present throughout the course. An independent second pass skimmed all questions and sampled technical/numeric assertions against source pages; this is not professional approval.

Excluded conflicting quiz values include propane explosion limits (LB2 text vs solution), blanket corrosion class C versus differentiated copper guidance (LB3), LPG cylinder 14/16 kg (LB5), old versus corrected combustion-air area/flow answers (LB6), AÜE threshold wording (LB7/finale), and medium-pressure testing 3 MPa versus 0.3 MPa (LB8). Do not silently choose between these documents for practical work. Historical BGB/insurance and old standards references remain document-orientation topics only. The course does not replace the original full diagram/table exercises.
